# v0.0.12 - Closure of the perimeter and administrative access

This document retains the version closure. Released versions are in [`releases.md`](../releases.md) and the roadmap is in [`next_releases.md`](../next_releases.md).

## Objective

Close the technical preparation of `https://assermetry.com` as a controlled environment. Under the mTLS general temporary rule, previously provided users must complete access via OIDC; Keycloak administrative routes must keep their mTLS rule permanent; and the maintenance lock must be able to close the entire hostname in one step.

The version does not allow self-registration or anonymous public access. Hetzner's origin only accepts HTTPS from Cloudflare and keeps all other public application ports closed.

The general temporary mTLS rule for non-administrative routes is retained in `v0.0.12` and `v0.0.13`. Controlled removal is part of `v0.0.14`.

## Version status

**Closed.** Phases 0 to 7 are completed and the stable baseline was recorded after passing the closing tests.

## Detail of phases

### Phase 0 - Inventory and baseline

**Objective**

Identify a recoverable version of Hetzner's deployment and check its operation before modifying manifests, DNS or firewall.

**Hecho**

- Images, manifests, PVC, secrets, contract, categories,
DNS and firewall rules.
- The commit and artifacts of the stable version were identified.
- SSH tunnel was tested for frontend, authentication, Light modes and
Blockchain, queues, workers, IPFS, validators, contract and categories.
- Base line of pods, reboots, disks, latencies and errors registered
  conocidos.

### Phase 1 - Adaptation of application and manifests

**Objective**

Prepare the `assermetry.com` configuration without altering active access by `localhost` or introducing productive values into common bases.

**Hecho**

- `local` and `prod` overlays retain `localhost`; `prod-domain`
contains only the domain configuration.
- Gateway separates the public issuer from the internal JWKS URL.
- Keycloak, Gateway and Ingress have consistent configurations for the
  dominio definitivo.
- `TrustNewsApi` remains confidential, with service account and secret outside
of the repository.
- Swagger, Redoc and OpenAPI are deactivated in production.
- The frontend retains relative routes `/backend` and `/auth`.
- The renderings and the local and productive flows by tunnel were left
validated without prematurely activating `prod-domain`.

### Phase 2 - Domain, Cloudflare and DNS

**Objective**

Register `assermetry.com`, delegate your DNS to Cloudflare and set up the external perimeter without exposing the source yet.

**Hecho**

- The domain was registered with automatic renewal, blocking of
transfer, contact privacy and 2FA on registrar and Cloudflare.
- The DNS was delegated to Cloudflare and the root record was proxified to
Hetzner; no `www` or mail services were published.
- Cloudflare uses TLS `Full (strict)`, HTTPS mandatory, TLS minimum 1.2 and
  TLS 1.3.
- Public resolvers return Cloudflare addresses, not direct IP
of origin.
- Hetzner maintained `80/tcp` and `443/tcp` filtered during this phase.
- SSH remains in `2222/tcp`, only with public key, no root access or
password authentication.

### Phase 3 - Temporary private access by customer certificate

**Objective**

Prevent domain access during preparation by a general mTLS rule and individual certificates per device.

**Hecho**

- An individual customer certificate was issued with the CA managed by
Cloudflare; the private key was generated and retained outside Git, Kubernetes, Hetzner and Cloudflare.
- The certificate and key were validated, packaged in PKCS#12 encryption and
were imported into the approved device.
- Cloudflare associates mTLS to hostname and the time rule covers `/`, `/auth`
and `/backend`.
- The tests without certificate were blocked and those carried out with the
valid certificate exceeded the mTLS control.
- Deactivation and reactivation of the rule as rollback was verified.
- No current revocation was executed or a replacement issue at this stage;
administrative revocation test is transferred to `v0.0.14`, during controlled removal of the temporary general mTLS.

### Phase 4 - TLS of Origin

**Objective**

Install a valid server certificate for the Cloudflare-Traefik jump before allowing HTTPS traffic to Hetzner.

**Hecho**

- A Cloudflare Origin CA was released, whose SAN includes `assermetry.com`.
- Certificate and key were installed manually in
`kube-system/trustnews-origin-tls`, outside the overlays and repository.
- `TLSStore/default` referencia el Secret productivo y Traefik presenta el
  certificado correcto.
- The cryptographic pair, chain, hostname, SNI and validity were validated by
tunnel without using `-k`.
- Frontend, Gateway and Discovery OIDC remained operational.
- Kind retains its local certificate and does not depend on the production material.
- Expiry is inventoryed, with prior warning and previous material
available for rollback. The renovation is manual.

### Phase 5 - WAF, restrictions and observability

**Objective**

Leave active and observable protections that must serve the withdrawal of the general mTLS.

**Hecho**

- Cloudflare maintains a permanent mTLS rule for
`/auth/admin`, `/auth/admin/*`, `/auth/realms/master` and `/auth/realms/master/*`.
- The Free Managed Rulet, custom rules for sensitive resources and
methods not allowed, and the limit rate of the higher cost endpoints were activated and tested.
- Traefik and Gateway apply a consistent limit of 5 MiB per request.
- OpenAPI remains inaccessible in production and internal services do not
  tienen Ingress ni NodePort.
- Gateway and Traefik generate structured logs without tokens, query strings or
  cuerpos. Fluent Bit incluye `kube-system`.
- Loki and Grafana have persistent storage, testing and data retention
and datasource after replacing your pods.
- Controlled rejections were correlated `401`, `403`, `429` and `500`
on the edge, Gateway and Loki.
- Phase 5 closure measurement showed 29 of 29 pods prepared, zero
Restarts and nine `Bound` PVC. The node was 5 % CPU, 78% memory and 56% disk; memory is under surveillance.

### Phase 6 - Definitive deployment still closed

**Objective**

Coordinated activation of `prod-domain` in K3s, aligning OIDC and Log-in with the final domain and validating the application without opening the source to the Internet.

**Hecho**

- `infra-prod` uses the domain overlay of Keycloak and
`apis-frontend-prod` uses the domain overlays of Gateway and Ingress.
- The technical realm remains `TrustNews`, shows `Assermetry` and publishes
the canonical issuer.
- `TrustNewsWeb` uses URLs, redirects, post-logout and Web Origin from
  `https://assermetry.com`.
- `TrustNewsApi` remains confidential, enabled and service account,
without altering or exposing their secret.
- All three Ingress use `host: assermetry.com` and the productive TLS continues
referenced by `trustnews-origin-tls`.
- The 29 pods were prepared, without reboots, and the nine PVC remained
  `Bound`.
- Frontend and discovery responded correctly with TLS validation; Gateway
rejected without credentials and accepted a valid JWT.
- OpenAPI returned `404` and the Smoke Light and Blockchain tests were completed.
- The non-empty default value of `ISSUE-002` was removed and confirmed that
It didn't match the productive secret.

### Phase 7 - Closure of the perimeter and administrative access

**Objective**

Complete the perimeter cloud and validate that the intended users access via OIDC after exceeding the mTLS general temporary rule. Administrative routes also retain its mTLS permanent rule and Cloudflare lock maintains a separate general lock.

**Hecho**

- Traefik uses `externalTrafficPolicy: Local` and only trusts headers
forwarded by the 22 official Cloudflare networks configured.
- Hetzner allows `TCP/443` exclusively from those networks. Direct access
to the origin of `443`, `80` and `6443` remains blocked.
- Cloudflare has the five custom rules occupied: overall lock,
mTLS administrative, mTLS general temporary, blocking of non-public resources and blocking of unexpected methods, in that order.
- The mTLS rule is active on the routes of
administration of Keycloak.
- The mTLS rule general temporary remains active and its controlled withdrawal remains
scheduled for `v0.0.14`.
- Under private access were verified frontend, discovery, login, refresh,
logout, roles, client credentials, Gateway with and without JWT and Light and Blockchain modes.
- The subsequent review kept all the workloads available, zero
restarts and the `Ready` node. Using memory close to 80% and warning `DNSConfigForming` remain as non-blocking observations.
- The maintenance lock is set up in first position and covers everything
`assermetry.com`. With valid user and administrator sessions, its activation produced `403` on both accesses and the events were attributed to the lock. By disabling it, both accesses returned to `200`; the final operating status was disabled and the permanent rules remained active.
- The `PROFILE=infra-prod` pipeline of the `8bdaa6f` commit completed the
reconciliación automatizada del realm `TrustNews` y del cliente `TrustNewsWeb`, obtuvo `keycloak_web_alignment=PASS` y confirmó el issuer canónico `https://assermetry.com/auth/realms/TrustNews`.
- The coexistence and attribution of Cloudflare rules was confirmed: a
administrative route without certification was blocked by the permanent administrative mTLS and a non-administrative route by the temporary general mTLS. The lock retains priority and general lock already tested. For `v0.0.14`, rollback consist of reactive only the temporary rule, leading the mTLS association and administrative rule.
- The usual network and valid customer certificate were checked on the
frontend, the OIDC discovery with the canonical issuer, login, the persistence of the session after reloading, the logout with return to login and the Light and Blockchain streams.
- With the valid customer certificate, Gateway refused
  `/backend/auth/is-admin` sin JWT mediante `403 Not authenticated`; tras
  renovar el token, aceptó el JWT y confirmó el rol `trust-admin` del usuario.
- The administrative routes were verified in both layers:
  certificado fueron bloqueadas por la regla mTLS administrativa; con un
  certificado válido, la consola de Keycloak respondió, mientras que su API
  administrativa sin token mantuvo el rechazo `401 Unauthorized`. El
  certificado no elude la autenticación de Keycloak.
- The stable base line of closure, captured the `2026-08-21T15:32:55Z` and

Systematic correction and its compliance tests belong to `v0.0.13`, consistent with the fact that functional correction is beyond the scope of this version.

## Closing criterion

The version closes when a provided user completes OIDC streams with a temporary client certificate, Gateway rejects protected requests without JWT, administration requires its permanent mTLS rule and Keycloak authentication, source only supports Cloudflare and lock blocks the entire platform. The general mTLS rule remains active and its withdrawal, along with proof of revocation of a disposable administrative certificate, is reserved for `v0.0.14`. `ISSUE-001` can remain mitigated and registered at closing; it must be resolved in `v0.0.13` before overcoming regression and repeatable demos.

## Out of reach

- Systematic testing and functional correction of GUI and API.
- Delivery of persistent credentials to clients.
- Pilots with real data or service commitments.
- Actual revocation of an administrative certificate; controlled proof is
will be performed in `v0.0.14` with a disposable certificate and another back up certificate.
- Full Hardening, backups, restoration and high availability.
- GO/NO-GO Decision for Public Production and Opening.
