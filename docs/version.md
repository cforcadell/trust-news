**En curso. Revisión: 2026-10-17, campaña publicada sobre `c84bb6e` y árbol sucio.** El flujo LIGHT publicó once casos de `Official Gold v0`: diez órdenes `VALIDATED` y una `VALIDATED_WITH_ERRORS`; el informe acredita 9/11 unidades e2e correctas, con una desviación de extracción y un timeout que bloqueó dos validadores. Released versions: [releases.md](releases.md). Later evolution: [next_releases.md](next_releases.md). Incremental execution plan: [working.md](working.md).

## Objective and limits

This version is a cumulative cycle of testing, discovery, implementation and new testing. It will end when the product is functionally stable according to the output criteria, not when completing a date or initial closed list.

The aim is to verify news and content statements generated by identifiable evidence, sufficient independence, explicit uncertainty and human review. The coincidence of LLMs and Blockchain registration do not prove the truth on their own.

The temporary gate of mTLS plus allowed names and the permanent administrative mTLS is maintained. No persistent customer accesses or presentation as production or high availability. The controlled opening and removal of the general mTLS belong to v0.0.14. X/Threads, new reputation, dedicated LLM and Cloudflare Tunnel are left out of this cycle.

## Forma de trabajo: campaña e2e publicada

Cada iteración empieza con una campaña `full --publish` LIGHT sobre los once casos activos, una repetición, revisión desplegada y hashes anotados; tras un fallo se reproduce su caso y se corrige un máximo de dos causas de plataforma. El análisis separa orden, afirmación, validador y fuente para evitar confundir 11 unidades con 33 respuestas. Después se publica de nuevo el caso afectado y el lote completo; el cierre necesita repetición estable en LIGHT y comprobación BLOCKCHAIN donde el contrato lo requiera. `gold-evidence`, `gold-domains` y pruebas locales sirven para aislar la etapa, no sustituyen la ruta publicada. Los problemas de configuración del gold o de recursos se anotan aparte sin contarlos como defectos de plataforma. Las pruebas y la secuencia operativa están en [working.md](working.md); los criterios por causa, en [issues.md](issues.md).

Una campaña con estado `COMPLETED_WITH_ERRORS`, veredicto erróneo, fuente primaria inaccesible o gold ambiguo abre o mantiene incidencias. Ningún estado `VALIDATED` por sí solo demuestra calidad factual. Los artefactos fechados, el commit/despliegue y los cambios sucios se registran antes de comparar campañas.

### Iteration 13.4 — Quality of evidence and stability of the routing

**In progress; highest factual-quality priority.** The canonical `context_id` citation contract rejects fabricated or non-citable model references (013). Origin URL and router metadata now reach Evidence Search, and `EXT_ONLY_OFFICIAL` filters retrieved results (021). Closed `routing-taxonomy-v1`, `route-v2` and new cache collections are implemented in the local cluster (022); deployment and cache measurements in Hetzner remain pending. These advances do not establish documentary independence:

- A source on another domain is currently labelled `INDEPENDENT`; this does not detect copies or two outlets repeating the same underlying report (021).
- `document_type` remains `UNKNOWN`, and the retriever does not provide auditable PDF page/fragment extraction for the primary report in the original regression case (021).
- Canonical citation identity proves that a model cited a delivered context; it does not prove that the context supports the claim or is independent (017, 021).
- The benchmark, viewer and offline `--analyze` runner now record stages, verdicts, errors, cited contexts and campaign summaries. `Official Gold v0` tiene ahora once casos activos, seis `TRUE` y cinco `FALSE`, tras retirar el falso de población. Las seis fuentes siguen `CANDIDATE_PENDING_INDEPENDENT_HUMAN_APPROVAL`. La campaña publish del 17 de octubre aporta diagnóstico e2e, no aceptación (017).

Complete PDF recovery, provenance/copy classification and the rule for claims attributed to a primary document before treating secondary evidence as decisive. Keep order `355f6090-cec0-4ed3-a29a-46763fe66cc6` as regression evidence: the submitted news cannot be its own decisive corroboration. Then validate route taxonomy and cache behavior on the deployed system. Independently approve and freeze the eleven active `Official Gold v0` cases before acceptance campaigns; define thresholds and add a separate abstention/`UNKNOWN` set before claiming benchmark quality.

The historical broad local baseline on 2026-09-08 was **199 passing Python tests**, excluding six external integrations. It has been superseded by the reproducible regression below; neither baseline attests factual quality or target deployment.

The smokes of September 4–5 completed LIGHT (23/23) and a BLOCKCHAIN repeat (26/26). There was no complete joint execution in green. On 2026-10-06, `tests/run-local-regression.sh` created a disposable environment from pinned dependencies, passed `pip check`, and completed **284 API tests plus 42 frontend unit tests**, including the concurrent validator-cache safety matrix for ISSUE-001. The same result was reproduced on 2026-10-15 against `b9003cf` plus the current working tree: `pip check` clean, **284 API tests and 42 frontend unit tests passing**. This supersedes the incomplete 2026-09-19 environment that lacked `hexbytes`. The browser runner now fails on horizontal overflow, rejects missing credentials before browser startup and preserves HTTP/loading/console diagnostics after early failures, but strict LIGHT and BLOCKCHAIN runs against the same deployed revision are still required to close ISSUE-016.

On 2026-10-15, the then-current `Official Gold v0` directory validated as **12 datasets / 12 assertions**; the active set on 2026-10-17 has **11 cases / 11 assertions** after a gold correction. The evaluation, viewer and LLM-benchmark selection passed **100 focused tests**; 99 passed inside the restricted sandbox and the single local-HTTP server test passed when repeated with loopback socket access. Ese resultado de tests verificó estructura y runner, no calidad factual; la primera campaña publish se registra arriba.

A local three-repetition `eu-official-statistics-2025-v1` evaluation on 2026-09-29 saved 27 validator results and five upstream HTTP 429 errors. Its recovered pipeline report counts 2 of 9 assertion/repetition units as end-to-end correct under the strict rule that any validator error or extraction failure fails the unit. The case has three synthetic assertions with no reviewed acceptable domains or reference sources; this result diagnoses the runner and current configuration, not population factual accuracy. The local artifacts remain under `tests/data/evaluation/artifacts/` and need durable retention before serving as release evidence.

Limitations of accumulated evidence:

- effective external integrations and proveedor/modelo without full baseline;
- temporary original artifacts, not persistent file;
- no previous PASS alone provides factual correction;

- new real cases are incorporated as reversed regressions.

## Exit criterion

- Zero P0 and zero P1 of Objective 13 open unless explicitly deferred with
cause, impact, responsible, mitigation and destination version.
- No isolation bypass, privilege elevation, data loss,
self-confirmation of documents and decisive results without eligible support.
- Full contract and regression suite without faults; GUI/API and a representative, human-reviewed factual corpus within thresholds agreed before the evaluation.
- Two consecutive full cycles without new P1 and without reopening corrections by
the same case; any new finding restarts this counter.
- Three consecutive demos with login, LIGHT and BLOCKCHAIN; at least one from another
red/equipo with registered temporary certificate, preparation and closure.
- Accountants, dates, errors, uncertainty and consistent evidence in API and GUI;
Mobile is only credited when there is no overflow at 390 px.
- Baseline, persistent ordnance, known limitations and decision-making
postponement available for review.
