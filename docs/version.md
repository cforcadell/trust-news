## Objective and limits

This version is a cumulative cycle of testing, discovery, implementation and new testing. It will end when the product is functionally stable according to the output criteria, not when completing a date or initial closed list.

The aim is to verify news and content statements generated by identifiable evidence, sufficient independence, explicit uncertainty and human review. The coincidence of LLMs and Blockchain registration do not prove the truth on their own.

The temporary gate of mTLS plus allowed names and the permanent administrative mTLS is maintained. No persistent customer accesses or presentation as production or high availability. The controlled opening and removal of the general mTLS belong to v0.0.14. X/Threads, new reputation, dedicated LLM and Cloudflare Tunnel are left out of this cycle.

## Working form of the version

Each iteration lasts from one to three days and limits implementation incidences to two. The cycle is:

1. **Note:** execute real cases, regression and probes; record environment,
review, entry and result.
2. **Classify:** create or reopen an incidence with proven cause, impact,
the closure criterion and the relationship with existing criteria.
3. **Priorize:** first P0; then P1 affecting safety, factual correction
or blocking evidence; then robustness, presentation and debt P2/P3.
4. **Implement:** correct a narrowed cause and amend any policy or
the contract whose change may alter stored results.
5. **Validate:** overcome the original case, focused tests and regression
affected; then check deployment and both modes where applicable.
6. **To accumulate:** to retain the case as regression and update the baseline.
new result can reopen, divide or reprove previous work.

An incidence does not change priority only by age. At each iteration start, severity, scope, diagnostic confidence, dependencies and ability to block further learning are reviewed. Findings that invalidate a verdict or a previous test are in front of visual work.

`version.md` retains the status of the version, iterations and summary evidence. `issues.md` retains causes and closure criteria. Reports for each execution must be dated artifacts, not new narrative documents that duplicate both files.

This attests local code behavior, not factual quality or deployment. There is no newer documented clean full-suite baseline.

Its recovered pipeline report counts 2 of 9 assertion/repetition units as end-to-end correct under the strict rule that any validator error or extraction failure fails the unit. The case has three synthetic assertions with no reviewed acceptable domains or reference sources; this result diagnoses the runner and current configuration, not population factual accuracy. The local artifacts remain under `tests/data/evaluation/artifacts/` and need durable retention before serving as release evidence.

Limitations of accumulated evidence:

- a single effective identity in the initial smoke;
- effective external integrations and proveedor/modelo without full baseline;
- temporary original artifacts, not persistent file;
- no previous PASS alone provides factual correction;
- new real cases are incorporated as reversed regressions.

## Exit criterion

- Zero P0 and zero P1 of Objective 13 open unless explicitly deferred with
cause, impact, responsible, mitigation and destination version.
- No isolation bypass, privilege elevation, data loss,
self-confirmation of documents and decisive results without eligible support.
- Full contract and regression suite without faults; GUI/API and a representative, human-reviewed factual corpus within thresholds agreed before the evaluation.
- Two consecutive full cycles without new P1 and without reopening corrections by
the same case; any new finding restarts this counter.
- Three consecutive demos with login, LIGHT and BLOCKCHAIN; at least one from another
red/equipo with registered temporary certificate, preparation and closure.
- Accountants, dates, errors, uncertainty and consistent evidence in API and GUI;
Mobile is only credited when there is no overflow at 390 px.
- Baseline, persistent ordnance, known limitations and decision-making
postponement available for review.
